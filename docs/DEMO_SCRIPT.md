# DVT Talent AI — 15-Minute Executive Demo Script
**Version:** 2.0.0 (October 2026)  
**Objective:** Demonstrate the dual-sided AI recruitment platform, proving how it solves talent acquisition friction for enterprise recruiters while empowering candidates with zero-fabrication career intelligence.

---

## 00:00 - 04:00 // Act 1: The Recruiter Command Center & Autonomous Swarm

- **Scene:** Log into `/dashboard` as an agency recruiter.
- **Talking Point:** *"Recruiters today waste 70% of their week manually Boolean-searching stale databases and writing cold outreach. DVT Talent AI deploys a specialized 5-agent Pydantic AI swarm that turns a raw requisition into a curated, verified talent pipeline."*
- **Action:**
  1. Open `/dashboard/jobs` and click **"Optimize JD"** on a Senior AI Engineer requisition. Show how the **Requisition Analyzer Agent** extracts required skill taxonomies and removes non-inclusive bias.
  2. Navigate to `/dashboard/swarm` and click **"Initiate Sourcing Swarm"**.
  3. Highlight the live **Neural Stream** as the **Candidate Sourcing Agent** and **Screening Agent** discover and evaluate candidates in real time.

---

## 04:00 - 07:00 // Act 2: Explainable Scoring & Human-in-the-Loop Copilot

- **Scene:** Open `/dashboard/copilot/[taskId]`.
- **Talking Point:** *"Autonomous doesn't mean black-box. We believe in human judgment at critical inflection points. Our Copilot pauses at three mandatory checkpoints."*
- **Action:**
  1. Show **Checkpoint 1 (JD Review)** with the optimized description.
  2. Advance to **Checkpoint 2 (Candidate Curation)**. Show the candidate score cards with explainable strengths, gaps, and qualification metrics.
  3. Highlight the **Outreach Review Gate**: demonstrate that email sequences cannot be sent without explicit recruiter sign-off.

---

## 07:00 - 11:00 // Act 3: The Candidate Portal & Career-Ops Engine

- **Scene:** Open `/portal` in a clean window as an active job seeker.
- **Talking Point:** *"Now let's see the other side of the equation. Why do candidates love this platform? Because we give them the Career-Ops Suite completely free."*
- **Action:**
  1. Show `/portal/jobs`. Demonstrate the **5-Dimension Fit Score** (Title, Tech Stack, Experience, Domain, Culture).
  2. Point out the **Ghost-Job Detection badge (Block G)**: *"Candidates never waste time applying to stale listings or compliance placeholders."*
  3. Click **"Tailor Resume"**: demonstrate the **Zero-Fabrication Engine** that optimizes keywords without hallucinating past achievements.
  4. Open `/portal/interview-prep`: demonstrate instant **STAR Behavioral Interview Flashcards**.
  5. Open `/portal/profile`: showcase the **Employer Privacy Shield**, demonstrating how candidates blacklist their current employer's domain from discovery swarms.

---

## 11:00 - 15:00 // Act 4: Enterprise Governance & Compliance

- **Scene:** Navigate to `/dashboard/compliance` and `/dashboard/settings`.
- **Talking Point:** *"Enterprise HR demands auditability. DVT Talent AI is built from the ground up for EEOC compliance, the EU AI Act, and enterprise security."*
- **Action:**
  1. Show the **AI Decision Ledger** with immutable decision records, confidence scores, and mandatory reviewer override notes.
  2. Run the **4/5ths Adverse Impact Calculator** demonstrating automated disparity checks across candidate pools.
  3. Show **Enterprise SAML SSO** (Okta, Azure AD) and **Bi-Directional ATS Sync** with Greenhouse and CEIPAL.
- **Closing:** *"DVT Talent AI aligns recruiters and candidates in a transparent, ethical ecosystem. Any questions?"*
